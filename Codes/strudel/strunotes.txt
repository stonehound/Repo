Notes(//Notes for Strudel music making with code//

Controlling REPL:
Crt & Enter , run code
Crt & + , stop code


Functions:
sound() , function that plays samples
sound(insect:0) , plays sample index 0 from sample set 'insect'
sound("bd hh sd oh").bank("RolandTR707") ,  Play a bass drum, high hat, snare drum, and open hat from a 707
sound("<bd hh sd oh>") , Plays each drum sample for a single 2(s) cycle, this would be a total of 8(s)
sound("<bd hh sd oh bd oh sd hh>*8") , this sample will pla 8 times faster, taking 2(s) to play
sound("bd - sd -") or sound("bd ~ sd ~") , either - or ~ acts as a rest

setcpm(120/4) , sets the ratio of cycles per minute, 120(c)/4(m) could work as 30BPM
 

Notes:
-Drum Machines can be loaded, examples such as AkaiLinn, RhythmAce, ViscoSpaceDrum
-All sounds played by the sound function fit within a cycle, which is predefine as 2(s) long
