//Notes for Strudel music making with code//

Controlling REPL:
Crt & Enter , run code
Crt & + , stop code


Functions:
setcmp(180/4) , set the cycles per minute, this would be 45CMP rather than the default 30CPM

sound() , function that plays samples
sound(insect:0) , plays sample index 0 from sample set 'insect'
sound("bd hh sd").bank("RolandTR707") , play bass drum, high hat, and snare samples from a Roland TR707 drum machine  
 - Note , adding more samples into the input of the sound() function changes the tempo to accomodate the preset 2 second Cycle every sound is fit into
sound("<bd hh sd oh>") , the <> causes every sample to be played for one Cycle, this would play over the course of 8 seconds
sound("<bd hh sd oh>*16") , This speeds up each cycle 16 times the normal speed, this would play over the course of .5 seconds
sound("bd - sd - oh") , use - or ~ as a rest
sound("bd [hh hh] bd [sn sn]") , use [] to create subsequences, fraction subdivisions of cycles per sample
